## Alternative Licenses

### **Apache 2.0**
- Similar to MIT but with patent protection
- More legal protection for businesses
- Slightly more complex
- Good for: Enterprise projects

### **GPL (GNU General Public License)**
- "Copyleft" - derivatives must also be open-source
- Stricter than MIT
- Good for: Pure open-source projects

### **AGPL**
- Like GPL but for network services
- Requirements apply to network use too
- Most restrictive
- Good for: Server-side SaaS applications

### **Proprietary/Commercial**
- Keep all rights
- Can license to others
- Good for: Commercial products

- Medical device certification

If storing real patient data, you need:
- Business Associate Agreements (BAA)
- Encrypted data storage
- Access logs
- Audit trails
